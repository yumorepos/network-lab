# W1.4 Version 2 baseline: the predictive scoreboard

**Purpose.** The frozen predictive-skill baseline. **Every later calibration
change is scored against the numbers in this document.**

**Frozen at.** commit `999f4f5` (`claude/network-lab-finalization`),
2026-08-14, working tree clean.

**Relationship to V1.** This does **not** replace `docs/W1.4_baseline_freeze.md`
(commit `bd7d4dd`), which remains the **artifact** baseline: did the outputs
change, and which markets moved. This document adds the third comparison type
that V1 explicitly could not support.

| comparison type | V1 artifact freeze | V2, this document |
|---|---|---|
| Did the artifacts change, and which markets moved? | **yes** | inherits V1 |
| Did model fit improve? | **yes** | inherits V1 |
| Did predictive skill on real launches improve? | **no** | **yes, for the cells listed below and no others** |

---

## 1. What this baseline may and may not be used to claim

Stated first, because the temptation to over-apply a scoreboard is the failure
mode this document exists to prevent.

**It supports:**

- A predictive claim about **WestJet YYC** launch agreement (Family 1) and
  incremental value over demand rank (Family 4), over three decision dates.
- A **right-sizing bias** claim for all three studies.
- A **descriptive** survival picture, dominated by seasonality.

**It does not support:**

- Any cross-study predictive claim.
- Any Alaska predictive claim. Alaska has **one** positive event.
- Any Porter claim carrying an interval. Porter has **one** decision date.
- Any survival claim with an interval, at any horizon, in any study.

**A change whose only evidence is an Alaska or Porter predictive metric cannot
be adopted on that basis.** A change that improves a WestJet Family 1 or Family
4 metric, or reduces right-sizing bias, can be scored here.

## 4. Known defects carried in this baseline

These are **in** the baseline. A later change that removes one is measured
against these numbers, not against a repaired version of them.

1. **D1, corridor-growth collapse.** Growth is a raw ratio of two single T-100
   years. At the 2023-01 cutoff it is 0.196 (YYC) and 0.166 (YYZ), collapsing
   median anchored WestJet demand from 13,156 to 2,023 and median margin to
   -3,829%. **The 2023-01 WestJet cell is dominated by this artifact.** It
   contributes 5 of WestJet's 15 positives.
2. **D2, right-sizing is a constant.** 461 of 463 decision rows choose 3x.
3. **The survey threshold is a fixed 4,000** while the true StatCan publication
   floor moves by vintage (280 to 3,090). Retained deliberately.
4. **Geography is 2023 at every cutoff.** Declared as a lookahead at the two
   cutoffs preceding the 2023-07 release.
5. **BEA and StatCan values are later revisions** of their reference years.
   Irreducible without archived vintages.
6. **The carrier brand map is not effective-dated.**
7. **Positive-unlabeled outcomes.** Unlaunched candidates are not negatives.
8. **Seasonality dominates the survival cohort**, and a seasonal stop is not a
   business failure.

## 5. How to score a future change against this

1. Re-run `.venv/bin/python -m replay.run_replay` and
   `.venv/bin/python -m replay.report` with the change in place.
2. Report the **WestJet Family 1 and Family 4** numbers before and after, with
   intervals, and the right-sizing ratios for all three studies.
3. Name every market that moves category, per the standing change-report rule.
4. A local fit improvement that does not move these numbers is **not** evidence
   of predictive improvement.
5. **An improvement inside an interval that spans zero is not an improvement.**

## 6. Unfreezing

This baseline is superseded when the replay universe changes materially: a new
decision date becomes mature (2025-01 becomes available for a 24-month outcome
once T-100 reaches 2027-01), a new study is added, or a defect in the replay
itself is found and fixed. Any of those requires a new freeze document and a
statement of what moved and why.

## 7. Reporting addendum, 2026-08-16: the per-date breakdown

**This section adds no number to the freeze and changes none.** Sections 1 to 6
above are the baseline as frozen at `d514ec2` and are not rewritten. This is a
pointer, added when independent review finding P1-2 was closed.

The protocol required a per-decision-date breakdown alongside the pooled
Family 1 and Family 4 results. It was not published with the first version of
this baseline. It now is, generated from `decision_rows.parquet` in
`docs/W1.4_replay_results.md`, section 3, under "Per-decision-date breakdown"
and "Leave-one-decision-date-out".

Two things in it bear directly on how section 3 of this document may be read,
and both are properties of the pooled numbers already frozen above rather than
revisions to them:

1. **The intervals in section 3 cluster by metro, not by decision vintage.**
   They describe sampling variation across metros with the three decision
   dates held fixed. A future change scored against this baseline could look
   significant on vintage variation alone. Rule 5 of section 5 should be read
   with that in mind.

2. **The pooled k=10 difference rests on a single decision date.** Removing
   2024-01 takes it to exactly zero. At k=20 both rankings reach recall 1.000,
   so the zero difference there is arithmetic saturation rather than a
   measured tie.

Neither observation changes the Family 4 conclusion, which was a null and
remains a null. Both change how narrowly it should be stated. The exact
figures live in the generated report, not here, per rule 6.
